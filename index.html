About Us

Exquisite uniforms for schools, businesses and institutions across Kenya.

Hawi Sartorial Ltd is a Nairobi-based fashion and design company dedicated to producing premium-quality uniforms and corporate apparel for schools, organizations, businesses, and institutions across Kenya.

Our Mission

Deliver refined garments that elevate brand identity, comfort and confidence for every client.

Our Vision

To be the trusted partner for premium uniforms and tailored apparel across East Africa.

Core Values

Craftsmanship, precision, integrity, reliability and exceptional customer care.

Our Story

Built on a passion for luxury craftsmanship, we merge tradition with modern manufacturing to create uniforms that last and impress.
